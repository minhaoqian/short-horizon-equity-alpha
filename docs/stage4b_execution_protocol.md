# Stage 4B — approved prespecified execution-assumption protocol

Approved before any portfolio performance, RL-065, 2026-10-06. Applies only to the executable benchmark simulation. Stage4A strict-identification artifacts and conclusions remain permanently separate; Stage1 five-day labels, Stage2 features and Stage3 forecasts unchanged.

- Entry: scheduled regular-session open t+1, only positive finite observed open. Otherwise assume no execution, cancel name/sleeve entry, keep intended allocation as cash. No chase, future reallocation or observed-open resizing of previously fixed shares.
- Exit: scheduled open t+6. If missing/nonpositive, retain position/assets/claims/short obligations; retry on each next global date in sequence. Execute at the first admissible positive regular-session open with verified owned asset/quantity. Exactly five subsequent global dates maximum, never five security rows or a best price.
- At cap failure: unresolved_execution, retain obligations and apply approved strict known-close halt; no extending cap, imputed open, close substitution or assumption of extinction. An earlier unresolved corporate-action quantity is an integrity/remaining-quantity gate, not a discretionary shortening of the price fallback.
- Record scheduled/assumed actual dates, global delay, reason and actual market-day holding duration. Market exits have duration5+delay; do not relabel them exactfive-day holdings. Corporate cash replacement and uncertain assets are separate states, not fabricated market trades.
- Keep predetermined signal-time ranks, fixed reference capital, capped allocations, gross flow rules, no cross-netting, cash/entitlement/split conventions and both candidate forecasts. Delayed positions/attempts retain their actual known share quantities and enter subsequent planned gross-flow calculations. No new universe or target-availability filtering.
- Opening-effective share transformations preserve owned quantities/obligations and existing entry<ex<=actualexit entitlement; entry-day rights excluded. Cash/share accounting retains prior-share basis for same-date actions. Unknown successor quantities cannot be inferred as zero from negative parent factors or from later prices; a positive successor open alone is not enough for liquidation.
- Development signals2003–2019 only. Narrow2020source access allowed only if actual development positions require runoff, never2020signals/eligibility/forecasts. If early permanent halt removes that need, do not read2020records.
- Run execution-feasibility before any PnL/Sharpe/candidate returns/selection. If infeasible, stop for review; never tune delay/constraints after outcomes. Label all results “portfolio simulation under prespecified execution assumptions”, never fully observed historical execution.

Implementation: src/portfolio/fallback.py, fallback_audit.py, fallback_report.py; scripts/33_stage4b_execution_feasibility.py. Its actual-order audit stops new orders at the first unresolved integrity/execution state, retains pre-submitted orders and existing positions, and records the operational state across the entire development calendar. It does not manufacture post-halt candidate trades to inflate full-period counts. No performance implementation authorized by an audit failure.
