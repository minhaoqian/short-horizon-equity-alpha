## Proposed deterministic convention — NOT implemented or frozen

For each cohort independently, let q be its verified signed parent quantity and e=0.535*q. Preserve the accepted ratio and separate any prior parent cash rights.

1. Simulated account elects HSBC ADS consideration for every affected position and submits valid exchange instructions at the first historically supported opportunity. The election is a prespecified account assumption, not a claim about an observed historical account.
2. Whole ADS component W=sign(e)*floor(abs(e)); signed fractional component F=e-W. Require W+F=e, abs(F)<1 and sign preservation. Negative quantities apply the same algebra to short delivery/cash obligations. No cross-cohort or long/short netting, no fractional ADS trading.
3. Keep W as pending successor inventory until the historically supported delivery/credit point D. Keep F as a distinct cash-in-lieu claim/obligation. Timely instruction submission alone does not establish D or immediate tradability. A short's symmetric delivery convention is a simulation assumption, not evidence of a particular lender election or actual borrow access.
4. Cash method M must be the documented issuer choice. If M is effective-date quotation, value F with the specified ADS closing quotation on the legal effective date, once that quotation and method are available; never a later substitute. If M is agent-sale proceeds, use documented net proceeds for the applicable fractional pool and effective availability; do not retrospectively value the claim using later realization at an earlier boundary. Unknown method/amount remains an explicit unmeasured claim, never zero.
5. Tradable inventory activates only when delivery is supported as effective before the relevant regular-session opening. If a source establishes a delivery date but no pre-open time, propose the next global trading opening as the earliest eligible attempt. Date-only evidence is not assumed to imply pre-open credit. This convention requires approval; no delivery date has been selected here.
6. Apply the unchanged Stage4B chronological first-admissible-opening rule from scheduled exit through exactly five subsequent global dates, additionally requiring deliverable inventory. The cap remains anchored to scheduled exit, not restarted at delivery. Undelivered inventory at cap or unmeasured remaining claims/obligations propagate unresolved state and the existing halt policy. Liquidating whole ADSs does not extinguish pending fractional cash or prior claims. No capital reuse on unresolved obligations.
7. Report entitlement, pending/delivered whole inventory, fractional cash claim, instruction/delivery evidence, scheduled/actual exit and global delay separately. Label all later results simulation under approved settlement/execution assumptions. Frozen Stage1 labels, Stage4A strict result and Stage4B originally failed audit remain unchanged.

D and M are unset evidence inputs, not tunable parameters. The convention is deterministic once supported inputs exist, but is not presently an executable dated baseline. Approval of account ADS election/signed decomposition cannot manufacture the issuer's choice or a delivery point. If the lead later authorizes a hypothetical cash method or dated delivery assumption, that must be separately labeled and prespecified rather than described as established history.
